Loy Krathong Day 

       Loy Krathong Day is one of the most popular festivals of Thailand. 
It celebrated annually on the Full-Moon Day of the Twelfth Lunar Month. It 
takes place at a time when the weather is fine as the rainy season is over and 
there is a high water level all over the country.  

The Krathong usually contains a candle, three joss-sticks, some flowers
